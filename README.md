## 🚀 What Is NeuzCore?

NeuzCore is a featherweight Roblox bootstrapper built for players who refuse to let aging hardware dictate their framerate. Born from the philosophy that smooth gameplay should never be a luxury reserved for expensive rigs, NeuzCore rethinks how your machine talks to Roblox — trimming overhead, taming background noise, and handing you the keys to a level of customization most launchers never bother to offer.

Think of it as a pit crew for your potato PC. Where the stock experience stumbles, NeuzCore sprints. It doesn't try to be everything to everyone — it focuses on doing one thing extraordinarily well: getting you into the game faster, with less lag, and with the controls to keep it that way.

Whether you're rocking integrated graphics, a decade-old laptop, or a modest budget build, NeuzCore treats your system with the respect a flagship deserves.

## 🎯 Why NeuzCore Exists

The Roblox ecosystem has grown incredibly rich over the years, but that growth has come with weight. For players on low-end machines, that weight translates directly into stutter, loading screens that feel like eternity, and a nagging sense that the game is running *around* them rather than *for* them.

NeuzCore was sculpted from a simple conviction: performance tuning should be accessible, transparent, and reversible. No black boxes. No permanent system modifications you can't undo. Just a lean layer of control that sits between you and the game, quietly doing the heavy lifting so your CPU doesn't have to.

## 🧠 Under the Hood — A Different Way to Think About Bootstrapping

Most bootstrappers behave like a bouncer: they stand at the door and check IDs. NeuzCore behaves more like an air traffic controller. It watches the runway, sequences what needs to happen, clears the path, and only then waves the plane through. The result is a launch sequence that feels less like waiting and more like arriving.

The architecture is deliberately modular. A lightweight core handles orchestration, while optional modules handle specialized tasks like process affinity tuning, network jitter smoothing, and shader cache management. Disable any module you don't need; the rest keep working. This means NeuzCore scales up with your ambitions and down with your hardware.

## 🏗️ Design Principles

1. **Lightness first.** Every feature must justify its weight. If it can't, it doesn't ship.
2. **Reversibility always.** Nothing is permanent unless you say so.
3. **Clarity over cleverness.** A setting you can't understand is a setting you can't trust.
4. **Accessibility as default.** Responsive layouts and multilingual strings aren't extras; they're baseline.
5. **Community-guided.** Feedback shapes the roadmap more than any internal wishlist.

## 🔐 Privacy and Trust
